# E-Commerce-Sales-Dashboard
📊 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard
🔹 Short Description / Purpose

The E-Commerce Sales Dashboard is an interactive Tableau visualization designed to analyze global sales performance, customer behavior, and operational trends. It enables businesses to track key metrics like sales, quantity, shipping costs, and payment preferences to support data-driven decision-making.

🛠️ Tech Stack
📊 Tableau Desktop – Data visualization and dashboard creation
📂 Data Preparation – Cleaning and structuring raw data
📈 Calculated Fields – Custom metrics and aggregations
🌍 Map Visualization – Geographic sales analysis
📁 File Format – .twb/.twbx and .csv
📁 Data Source

The dataset used is a global e-commerce sales dataset, containing transaction-level data such as:

Sales amount
Quantity sold
Shipping cost
Payment mode
Region and country
Product category and time (year/month)

The dataset is structured to enable analysis of sales trends, regional performance, and customer purchasing behavior.

🌟 Features / Highlights
📌 Business Problem

E-commerce businesses generate large volumes of sales data, but often lack a clear and interactive way to:

Track regional sales performance
Understand customer payment preferences
Monitor shipping costs and pricing trends

Key questions addressed:

Which regions contribute the most to sales and quantity?
What are the most preferred payment methods?
How do shipping costs vary over time?
What are the trends in product pricing across years?
🎯 Goal of the Dashboard

To build an interactive analytics tool that:

Monitors overall sales and quantity performance
Analyzes regional contribution to sales
Tracks customer behavior by payment mode
Identifies trends in shipping cost and product pricing

PayPal

👉 Insight: Digital payments dominate customer transactions.

🗺️ Sales by Country (Map Visualization)

Geographic map showing sales distribution across countries like the US, UK, India, Brazil, etc.

👉 Insight: Helps identify high-performing and emerging markets.

🚚 Month-wise Shipping Cost (Line Chart)

Tracks shipping cost trends across months.

👉 Insight: Shipping costs fluctuate, with peaks in certain months indicating seasonal demand or logistics impact.

📈 Unit Price Trend by Year & Category (Area Chart)

Shows pricing trends across years (2023–2025) and product categories.

👉 Insight: Product pricing shows variation over time, helping in pricing strategy decisions.

📈 Business Impact & Insights
Sales Optimization: Identify top-performing regions and markets
Customer Insights: Understand preferred payment methods
Cost Management: Monitor and control shipping cost fluctuations
Pricing Strategy: Analyze trends to optimize product pricing
Global Expansion: Identify potential regions for growth
