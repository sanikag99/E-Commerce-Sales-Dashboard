# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard  

## 🔹 Overview  
The **E-Commerce Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Tableau visualization designed to analyze global sales performance, customer behavior, and operational trends. It enables businesses to track key metrics such as **sales, quantity, shipping costs, and payment preferences** to support data-driven decision-making.



## 🛠️ Tech Stack  
- 📊 **Tableau Desktop** – Data visualization and dashboard development  
- 📂 **Data Preparation** – Data cleaning and structuring  
- 📈 **Calculated Fields** – Custom metrics and aggregations  
- 🌍 **Map Visualization** – Geographic sales analysis  
- 📁 **File Formats** – `.twb`, `.twbx`, `.csv`  



## 📁 Data Source  
The dataset is a **global e-commerce sales dataset** containing transaction-level data, including:  
- Sales amount  
- Quantity sold  
- Shipping cost  
- Payment mode  
- Region and country  
- Product category  
- Time (year/month)  

This dataset enables analysis of **sales trends, regional performance, and customer purchasing behavior**.



## 🌟 Features & Highlights  

### 📌 Business Problem  
E-commerce businesses generate large volumes of data but often lack an effective way to:  
- Track regional sales performance  
- Understand customer payment preferences  
- Monitor shipping costs and pricing trends  

### ❓ Key Questions Addressed  
- Which regions contribute the most to sales and quantity?  
- What are the most preferred payment methods?  
- How do shipping costs vary over time?  
- What are the trends in product pricing across years?  



## 🎯 Goal of the Dashboard  
To build an interactive analytics tool that:  
- Monitors overall **sales and quantity performance**  
- Analyzes **regional contribution to sales**  
- Tracks **customer behavior by payment mode**  
- Identifies trends in **shipping costs and product pricing**  



## 📊 Walkthrough of Key Visuals  

### 🔢 Key KPIs  
- **Total Sales:** 484,559  
- **Total Quantity:** 7,115  



### 🌍 Percentage of Quantity by Region (Pie Chart)  
- North America (~28%)  
- Asia Pacific (~27%)  
- Europe (~25%)  

👉 **Insight:** Sales are globally distributed, with strong contributions from North America and Asia Pacific.



### 💳 Customer Count by Payment Mode (Bar Chart)  
- Credit Card (Highest)  
- PayPal  
- Cash on Delivery  
- Bank Transfer  

👉 **Insight:** Digital payment methods dominate customer transactions.



### 🗺️ Sales by Country (Map Visualization)  
Shows sales distribution across countries such as the **US, UK, India, and Brazil**.  

👉 **Insight:** Helps identify high-performing and emerging markets.



### 🚚 Month-wise Shipping Cost (Line Chart)  
Tracks shipping cost trends across months.  

👉 **Insight:** Shipping costs fluctuate due to seasonal demand and logistics variations.



### 📈 Unit Price Trend by Year & Category (Area Chart)  
Shows pricing trends from **2023 to 2025** across product categories.  

👉 **Insight:** Useful for optimizing pricing strategies over time.



## 📈 Business Impact & Insights  
- 📌 **Sales Optimization** – Identify top-performing regions and markets  
- 👥 **Customer Insights** – Understand payment preferences  
- 💰 **Cost Management** – Monitor shipping cost fluctuations  
- 📊 **Pricing Strategy** – Optimize product pricing decisions  
- 🌍 **Global Expansion** – Identify potential growth regions
